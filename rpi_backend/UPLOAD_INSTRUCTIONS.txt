To update the RPI backend with camera stream support:

1. On your Windows machine, copy the api_server.py file to RPI:
   scp rpi_backend/api_server.py justinpulanco23@192.168.254.199:~/Downloads/rpi_backend/

2. On RPI, restart the server:
   cd ~/Downloads/rpi_backend
   python3 api_server.py

3. Test the camera endpoint:
   curl http://localhost:5000/api/camera/test

The updated api_server.py includes:
- /api/camera/test - Test camera functionality
- /api/camera/stream - Live MJPEG stream
